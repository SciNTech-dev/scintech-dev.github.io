Privacy at a glance

Your videos stay on your phone.

Loop search, stabilization, blending, audio processing, and rendering all happen on the device. Video files are never uploaded for processing.

Local media

The work happens where the video lives.

The app opens only the clip you choose through Android’s picker, open-file, or share flow. Temporary processing files and the finished render remain on the device.

SELECT

You choose the clip

The app does not request broad read access to your photo and video library.

PROCESS

Frames stay local

Analysis, motion processing, blending, audio work, and encoding run on the phone.

SAVE OR SHARE

You choose the destination

Save the finished MP4 directly to your gallery (Movies/Seamless Video Looper/) with one tap, or pass it to Android’s share sheet.

Network use

Reporting is separate from video processing.

The app uses Firebase (with Firebase App Check via Play Integrity) for the records below, and Google Play In-App Review for optional store review prompts. Source and rendered video files are never uploaded. Every automatic report type is gated under broad category switches in Settings (on by default), and a human reads every report and acts on it.

Feedback

Sent only when you tap Send from the Send Feedback button at the bottom right of the screen. Basic app, device, workflow, quality, and identifier fields are always included. Attachments under “Include app data” (including Screenshot) are ticked by default and can show your picture/video if on screen — expand and untick any item before sending.

Crash & exit reports

Crash stack traces, out-of-memory kills, ANR exits, and unexpected background exits are sent automatically to help diagnose failures. On by default; gated under broad category switches in Settings.

Hardware Test reports

Device capabilities, memory checks, and per-backend motion/interpolation timings from the on-device Hardware Test. No video or frames. On by default; gated under broad category switches in Settings.

Render-failure reports

When a render fails, stalls, scores poorly on seam quality, or shows signs of AI artifacts, the app reports the technical measurements and failure stage. No video, frames, or screenshots. On by default; gated under broad category switches in Settings.

Rating & shared-result records

When you share a render or tap an inline star rating on a result card, the app sends technical metadata about the clip, candidate/result summaries, trim range, preset, chosen settings, and any star rating. No video is attached. Gated under broad category switches in Settings.

Every record above also includes the language the app is showing you (from its 26 shipped languages). If your device asked for a different language than what is displayed — say, a language we do not yet support, or an in-app language override — that is included too, so translation effort goes where users are and language-specific issues get caught.

Identifiers and storage

Two identifiers, indefinite retention, 30-day deletion.

Firebase creates an anonymous app identifier (uid), used with Android ID to group related reports. The app contains no advertising or general usage-analytics SDK.

Submitted records are stored in Firebase Firestore and feedback screenshots in Firebase Storage. Reports are kept indefinitely (no automatic server-side expiration) unless you request deletion, and a human reads every report and acts on it. We delete your reports within 30 days of an email request. On your device, the local screenshot cache expires after 30 days.

Google Play separately handles installation, updates, In-App Review prompts, Play Integrity (used by Firebase App Check), and store-level data under Google’s privacy policies.

Your choices

Control what you send.

  • Every automatic report category (crash/exit reports, Hardware Test reports, render-failure reports, and rating/shared-result records) starts on by default and can be switched off under broad category switches in Settings.
  • Before sending feedback, expand “Include app data” to review default-ticked attachments and untick Screenshot or any other item you do not want to send.
  • Use the app’s Send Feedback button at the bottom right of the screen, or email us via Contact and deletion requests below, for privacy or deletion requests.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-24

On-device video processing

Seamless Video Looper analyzes, stabilizes, blends, processes audio, and renders videos on your Android device. Source videos and rendered videos are never uploaded to Firebase or any server for processing or reporting.

Video access and saving

You select a video through Android’s system picker, open-file action, or share action. The app receives access to that selected file rather than broad read access to your media library. Finished MP4 loops can be saved to your gallery (Movies/Seamless Video Looper/) with one tap or shared through Android’s share sheet.

Reports, startup services, and technical records

On startup, the app initializes Firebase, Firebase App Check (using Google Play Integrity to verify that requests come from the genuine app), and Firebase Anonymous Authentication. The app also integrates Google Play In-App Review so Android may prompt you to rate the app on Google Play. The app uses Firebase Firestore for feedback and technical reports, and Firebase Storage for attached feedback screenshots. Every automatic and technical report type is on by default and gated under broad category switches in Settings. A human reads every report and acts on it.

  • Feedback: sent only when you tap Send from the Send Feedback button at the bottom right of the screen. Every feedback report always includes your message, app version, device model, Android version, hardware tier, current workflow step, quality metrics, algorithm summary, memory-gate summary, active background work status, display/requested/default languages, test-lab flag, Android ID, and Firebase anonymous uid. Under “Include app data”, optional items (device & video metadata, settings, redacted logs, and Screenshot) are ticked by default; expand and untick any item you want to omit before sending.
  • Feedback screenshot: the Screenshot option is ticked by default and can show your picture/video if on screen — untick it before sending to leave it out. Attached screenshots are stored in Firebase Storage. The local screenshot cache on your device expires after 30 days.
  • Crash and process-exit reports: include crash stack traces, thread names, out-of-memory kills, ANR exits, non-crash unexpected exits, memory state, and device information. On by default; gated under broad category switches in Settings.
  • Hardware Test reports: include component outcomes, backend availability, timings, device details, memory checks, and short error information from the on-device Hardware Test. No video or frames. On by default; gated under broad category switches in Settings.
  • Render-failure and quality-failure reports: when a render fails outright (render_failure) or when the app detects stalled progress, low measured seam quality, or suspected AI-interpolation artifacts (failure), the report includes the failure type/stage, timing and quality measurements, blend method, and device information. No video, frame, or screenshot is included. On by default; gated under broad category switches in Settings.
  • Rating and shared-result records (rating): submitted when you share a completed render or tap an inline star rating on a result card (without sharing). May include video dimensions, frame rate, duration, codec and audio presence; cut, trim range, preset name, candidate/result summaries (allResults, allCandidates), score, blend and audio choices; quality and motion results; processing time; app and device information; and your star rating. No video file is attached. Gated under broad category switches in Settings.

Every report above also includes the language the app is currently displaying (from its 26 shipped languages) as a BCP-47 tag (for example, en-US). When that differs from what your device or the app requested, the requested language and your device’s default language are included as well.

Identifiers and accounts

No user-created account is required. Firebase creates an anonymous app-account identifier (uid). Records also include Android ID so related submissions from the same device can be grouped and deduplicated. These identifiers are not used as advertising IDs and are not intentionally tied to your name or email.

Advertising, analytics, and Google Play services

The app does not include advertising SDKs, general usage-analytics SDKs, or tracking pixels. Firebase App Check (Play Integrity) and Google Play In-App Review communicate with Google Play services under Google’s privacy policies. Google Play also separately processes store-level installation and update data under Google’s privacy policies.

Storage and retention

Submitted records are stored in Firebase Firestore, and attached feedback screenshots are stored in Firebase Storage. Reports are retained indefinitely (no automatic server-side expiration) unless you request deletion. A human reads every report and acts on it. On your device, the local screenshot cache expires after 30 days.

Permissions

The app uses foreground-service, notification, network-state, internet, wake-lock, boot-receiver, and Google-services permissions to render in the background and deliver the reporting functions described above. On Android 8.0 and 9 (API 26–28), WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE is actively used when you tap Save to gallery to write finished MP4 files to Movies/Seamless Video Looper/. Media selection itself uses Android’s system picker.

Your controls

  • Every automatic and technical report category (crash/exit reports, Hardware Test reports, render-failure reports, and rating/shared-result records) is gated under broad category switches in Settings.
  • Before sending feedback, expand “Include app data” to review default-ticked attachments and untick Screenshot or any other item you do not want to send; the screenshot option is ticked by default and can show your picture/video if on screen.

Contact and deletion requests

Use the app’s Send Feedback button at the bottom right of the screen, or email scintech.dev@gmail.com for privacy questions and requests concerning submitted data.

To have all reports Seamless Video Looper collected from your device deleted, email scintech.dev@gmail.com with the approximate date(s) you used the app or sent feedback and your device model so we can match your records. We delete every matching report across all report types (feedback, crash/exit, Hardware Test, render-failure, and rating/shared-result records) and any attached screenshots within 30 days of an email request.
