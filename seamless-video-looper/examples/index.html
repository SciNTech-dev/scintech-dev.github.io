A closer look

From clip to continuous moment.

Real screens from the current app: Easy mode’s two-step flow, Advanced mode’s cut, seam, and render controls, the Hardware Test report, and a side-by-side video of a raw repeat against the app’s seamless export.

Easy mode

Pick a clip. Get a scored loop.

Easy mode searches and renders scored loop options automatically. Its results screen rates the best loop — here, Loop quality Good at 87%, AI-smoothed — and offers Save to gallery and Share, with a star rating for the result.

Advanced controls

Shape the seam yourself.

Advanced mode exposes the full workflow: pick a clip and a preset, search for loop points, compare the scored candidates or set a custom cut, choose among five seam methods and the stabilization options, then render.

Hardware Test

Fit the work to the device.

Hardware Test scores the device and lists which motion and interpolation backends work on it, so the app can pick settings the hardware can actually run.

These two captures come from an Android emulator — the report names the emulator model, sdk_gphone64_x86_64 — so read them as illustrations of the screen. On your phone, the same report names your own device.

Footage guide

Start where motion can continue.

A good loop is already latent in the scene. The app helps you find and finish it.

GOOD START

Water, fire, clouds, foliage

Continuous organic motion gives the seam room to disappear.

TRY A FEW CUTS

Drift and slow zoom

Alignment can help when the camera moves gently through the shot.

MORE DIFFICULT

Crossings and occlusion

A person or object crossing the seam can double, bend, or reveal the transition.

A useful rhythm

Preview more than one answer.

A high score is a strong starting point, but the right loop depends on the scene. Watch the motion, listen to the sound, and choose the candidate that feels natural.
