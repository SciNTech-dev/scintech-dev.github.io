Compatibility

What each app requires.

Watermark Remover and Seamless Video Looper require Android 8.0 or later; Pic2Ink requires Android 7.0 or later. Full requirements, supported hardware, and answers to common questions are on each app’s compatibility page below.

Compatibility by app

Android 8.0+

Watermark Remover

  • Android 8.0 (API 26) or later, 64-bit ARM (arm64-v8a).
  • Photos only — no video. Saves to Pictures/WatermarkRemover.
  • One-photo AI model needs ~1.2 GB free RAM (included in the Google Play Full build).
  • Built-in Hardware Test tunes thread count, memory budget, and MI-GAN repair backends.

Android 8.0+

Seamless Video Looper

  • Android 8.0 (API 26) or later.
  • Decodes formats supported by your device’s Android media stack; outputs H.264 MP4 + AAC-LC audio.
  • Saves to gallery (Movies/Seamless Video Looper/) or shares via Android’s share sheet.
  • Built-in Hardware Test checks memory, decoding, optical flow, and AI interpolation availability.
Compatibility & FAQ Google Play ↗
Android 7.0+

Pic2Ink

  • Android 7.0 (API 24) or later.
  • NFC required only for NFC frames; Wi-Fi required for Bigme F7 Pro and TV/smart-display casting; PNG export needs neither.
  • 53-device catalog (31 NFC, 1 Wi-Fi, 21 file export); tested on TapMora Spectra 6 and Bigme F7 Pro.
  • Not for Kindle, Kobo, or other general e-readers.

Hardware adaptation

Watermark Remover and Seamless Video Looper each include an on-device Hardware Test that checks memory, decoding, and AI backends before picking defaults. Pic2Ink reads an NFC frame’s own configuration reply before sending and publishes its full tested-vs-untested 53-device catalog.
