Seamless Video Looper

Searches a clip for the frame where its ending can pass for a beginning, then renders a loop that keeps playing forward. The search and the render run on the phone.

Pic2Ink

Prepares a photo for an e-ink frame’s exact pixels and few inks, and sends it from the phone over NFC or Wi-Fi, casts to TVs, or exports PNG. Tested so far: TapMora Spectra 6 and Bigme F7 Pro.

Offline, and free

Each app runs its core work on the phone without a connection — free, with no ads and no accounts. Photos and videos are never uploaded to be processed.

Real science, named

Each app puts established methods to work on one practical problem, and says which ones.

Honest about what won’t work

Each app names the cases it handles badly: fully opaque marks or single-photo estimates that leave a faint outline for Watermark Remover, fast crossings and occlusion for Seamless Video Looper, and frames not yet tested for Pic2Ink.

A note from the SciNTech team

Most of what we build starts with something that is already yours. A photo you have every right to look at, with a pattern stamped across it that was never part of the picture. A clip of water or leaves that was almost a loop, if only its last frame knew how to become its first. A favorite photograph waiting to sit on the e-ink frame on your desk, rendered in that panel’s exact pixels and inks without a cloud account in the middle.

We don’t invent those moments back or lock them behind subscriptions. We look for what the picture, the clip, or the panel already says about itself: the mark that repeats while the photo underneath does not, the frame where the motion was already circling home, the pixels a bezel hides and the true colors a panel’s inks can show. Then we do the work right on the phone in your hand, offline, and we tell you plainly where it did not work.

That is the whole of it: careful science, made into tools that work wherever you are.

— The SciNTech team
