The apps

Two apps, built the same way.

Both run their analysis on the phone, both check the hardware before choosing defaults, and both say what they cannot do.

Beta

Watermark Remover

Learns a repeating, tiled watermark from the photo itself and lifts it off, without uploading the photo. A single standalone logo is declined rather than guessed at.

Seamless Video Looper

Searches a clip for the frame where its ending can pass for a beginning, then renders a loop that keeps playing forward. The search and the render run on the phone.

What we hold to

Three rules both apps keep.

Five values in full, each with an example from each app, are on the About page.

On the phone, not a server

Photos and videos are processed on the device. Neither app uploads them to do its work.

Your attention stays yours

The current releases have no ads, no subscription and no in-app purchase.

Honest about what won’t work

Each app names the cases it handles badly: a single logo for Watermark Remover, fast crossings and occlusion for Seamless Video Looper.

A note from the SciNTech team

Most of what we build starts with something that is already yours. A photo you have every right to look at, with a pattern stamped across it that was never part of the picture. A clip of water or leaves that was almost a loop, if only its last frame knew how to become its first.

We don’t invent those moments back. We look for what the picture or the clip already says about itself: the mark that repeats while the photo underneath does not, the frame where the motion was already circling home. Then we do the work where the photo and the clip already live — on the phone in your hand — and we tell you plainly where it did not work.

That is the whole of it: two small instruments for seeing a moment the way it was.

— The SciNTech team
