Privacy

Nothing here is watching.

This page covers the website. Each app has its own privacy page, linked below.

This website

  • The pages set no cookies and load no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
  • Fonts, styles and scripts are served from this site; nothing is loaded from a third party, and nothing is embedded.
  • If you press the theme button, your choice of light or dark is stored in your own browser’s local storage. It is never sent anywhere.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives the technical data any web server receives, such as your IP address; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

The apps

Each app processes photos and videos on the phone. What an app does send — feedback you choose to send, crash reports, hardware check results — is described in full on each app’s own page.
