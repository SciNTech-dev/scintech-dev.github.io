Join the test

Help Pic2Ink reach Google Play.

Before an app from a personal developer account can be published, Google requires a closed test: at least 12 testers, opted in for 14 days in a row. Pic2Ink is at that stage.

What you need

  • An Android phone with Android 7.0 or later, and the Google account you use on it.
  • Ideally NFC and an e-ink frame from the catalogue. Any phone can still test the app’s screens and the PNG export.
  • Keep the app installed for 14 days. Feedback goes through the app’s Send feedback button.

Three steps

  1. 1

    Join the testers group

    Join with the Google account you use on the phone. Google Play lets the group’s members into the test.

    Join the testers group ↗
  2. 2

    Opt in on Google Play

    Open Pic2Ink’s opt-in page on Google Play, signed in with the same Google account, and become a tester.

    Opt in on Google Play ↗
  3. 3

    Install from Google Play

    After opting in, Google Play offers the test version to install.

What testers send

Only the feedback you send.

Pic2Ink sends no automatic reports. A feedback report goes only when you press Send; its screenshot is ticked by default and shows your picture if it is on screen, so untick it to leave it out.
