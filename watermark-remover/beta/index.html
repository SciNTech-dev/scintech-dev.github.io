Join the beta

Help it see more photos clearly.

Watermark Remover is in a closed test on Google Play. Three steps get it onto your phone, and your reports shape what it learns to handle next.

Three steps

  1. 1

    Join the testers group

    Google Play lets a group’s members into the test. The group’s address still carries the name of our other app, Seamless Video Looper: it is the same team.

    Join the testers group ↗
  2. 2

    Opt in on Google Play

    Open the opt-in page signed in with the same Google account you used to join the group, and become a tester.

    Opt in on Google Play ↗
  3. 3

    Install from Google Play

    After opting in, the Play listing offers the test version to install.

    Install from Google Play ↗
What testers send

Reports, never photos.

Test builds share two kinds of results by default: a daily self-check run on sample images built into the app, and the phone’s hardware check. Each has its own switch in Settings. No photo of yours is included in any report.

Something wrong or missing? Tap the feedback button in the corner of the screen. That is the whole support channel; there is no email.
