Compatibility & FAQ

What it needs, and what it asks.

A 64-bit Android phone (8.0+), photos only (no video), and a little patience for high-resolution jobs. The rest is below.

Quick facts

Read from the app’s source code; current for version 0.8.0.
TopicWhat holds
Android & CPUAndroid 8.0 (API 26) or later on a 64-bit ARM (arm64-v8a) device.
Photos inPhotos only — no video. Images chosen in the Android picker, shared from another app, or opened via “Open with” (including .png and .cmt watermark files).
Photos outSaved at full resolution to Pictures/WatermarkRemover on Android 8.0 and later (PNG for Easy mode and Workbench “Save all”; JPEG or PNG for single-photo saves in Quick Remove and the Workbench viewer), or shared as PNG.
PermissionsNo broad access to your photo library: the app sees only the images you pick or share. On Android 8.0–9 it requests legacy storage write permission (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) to save to Pictures/WatermarkRemover; on Android 13+ it requests POST_NOTIFICATIONS for long-job progress notifications. Where it runsOn the phone. Learning and removal need no connection.
Hardware TestMeasures band thread counts, learn memory budget, and MI-GAN repair execution providers on your phone. It is the first item in the ⋮ menu on every screen.
Fill & AI modelsGoogle Play distributes only the Full build, which includes the on-device MI-GAN fill model and the one-photo neural model. A Lite build uses a classical fill instead and does not include the one-photo model.

Questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. Learning and removing the mark run on the phone and need no connection. A report you send while offline waits on the phone and is sent later.

Does it work on video?

No. Watermark Remover handles photos only — no video. If you pick a video file, Easy mode tells you to add photos instead.

Can it remove a single logo?

Yes, with limits. The on-device one-photo neural model estimates the mark and undoes it (and serves as a fallback for batches of up to 6 photos when no shared mark is found). On our 300-image test set the median improvement was +6.5 dB, with some modest results (examples). It needs about 1.2 GB of free memory and the Full build (Google Play distributes only the Full build). When you have several photos carrying the same logo, learning from 8 photos and saving the mark ended closer in 10 of 11 synthetic single-mark cases (two photos in 8 of 11), so check the preview before saving (why).

Why does the first analysis take a while?

Learning and reconstruction run arithmetic over every pixel under the mark on your phone. Expect a few minutes per image on most phones, and longer for large photographs — you can cancel and keep whatever has finished. The one-photo neural model takes about 20 seconds per 12-megapixel photo on a 2025 flagship phone and about 100 seconds on a 2020 mid-range phone.

Why does fine detail under the mark look painted?

Where the mark hid the picture completely, nothing of the original is left to recover, so that area is filled in from its surroundings: by the on-device MI-GAN model in the Full build distributed on Google Play (or by a classical fill in a Lite build). That area is a reconstruction, not the original.

What leaves the phone?

No photo and no result. Automatic and technical reports — crash, out-of-memory, ANR stack, interrupted-job, removal-failure, Hardware Test, self-test, result ratings, Advanced comparison choices, and save/share actions — carry no photos and are gated under broad category switches in Settings. Feedback is sent only when you press Send; its four attachment checkboxes (including Screenshot) are ticked by default and can show your picture/video if on screen — untick before sending to leave them out. Opening the app makes no network connection of its own. Reports carry Firebase uid, Android ID, and a random install_id, are retained indefinitely, read by a human who acts on them, and deleted within 30 days of an email request. The privacy policy lists every field.

Is it free?

Yes. Watermark Remover is free — no ads, no accounts, and nothing to buy — and works offline.

How do I send feedback?

Tap the feedback button in the corner of the screen, or choose Send feedback in the ⋮ menu on any screen. Its four attachment checkboxes (Image details, App logs, Screenshot, and Current settings) are ticked by default, and Screenshot can show your picture/video if on screen — untick any item you want to leave out before sending. A human reads every report and acts on it.

Where do I get it?

On Google Play, where it is listed as Watermark Remover (Offline). It has been available to everyone there since October 15, 2026; before that it was a closed test.

Can I remove any watermark?

The app restores photos. Whether you may alter a particular photo is a question of the photographer’s or owner’s rights, which the app cannot judge for you. Use it on photos you have the right to restore.
