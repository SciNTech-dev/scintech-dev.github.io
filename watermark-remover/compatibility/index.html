Compatibility & FAQ

What it needs, and what it asks.

An Android phone, a photo with a repeating mark, and some patience on the first run. The rest is below.

Questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. Learning and removing the mark run on the phone and need no connection. A report you send while offline waits on the phone and is sent later.

Can it remove a single logo?

No. The app learns the mark by comparing its copies, so it needs a mark that repeats across the photo. With a single standalone logo there is nothing to compare, and it declines (why).

Why does the first analysis take a while?

Before it removes anything, the app learns the mark from the photo itself, and that is the slow part. The app’s own estimate is about a minute on mid-range phones for the first analysis; that is an estimate, not a measured average across phones.

Why does fine detail under the mark look painted?

Where the mark hid the picture completely, nothing of the original is left to recover, so that area is filled in from its surroundings: by the MI-GAN model on the phone in the standard build, or by a classical fill in the lite build. That area is a reconstruction, not the original.

What leaves the phone?

No photo and no result. The app can send reports, each with its own switch in Settings: feedback you write and choose to send (a screenshot only if you attach one), crash and memory reports (on by default), hardware check results (on by default), and, in test builds, a daily self-check run on sample images built into the app. The privacy policy lists every field.

Is it free?

Yes, the current release is free, with no ads, no subscription and no in-app purchase.

How do I send feedback?

Tap the feedback button in the corner of the screen, or go to Settings → Send feedback. The report attaches the technical details needed to look into a problem, and you can add a screenshot. There is no support email; the app is the channel.

What does beta mean here?

The app is in a closed test on Google Play. You join the testers group, opt in on Google Play, and install from the Play listing — the steps are on the beta page. Google Play requires at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days before an app can apply for production.

Can I remove any watermark?

The app restores photos. Whether you may alter a particular photo is a question of the photographer’s or owner’s rights, which the app cannot judge for you. Use it on photos you have the right to restore.
