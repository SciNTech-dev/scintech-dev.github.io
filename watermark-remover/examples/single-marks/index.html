Examples

One logo, one stamp.

A mark that appears once per photo, learned from 8 other photos that carry it, saved, then removed from two new pictures. Every example is synthetic, so the true clean image exists and each result is scored against it.

Single marks, with a saved mark

A single logo, stamp or credit, saved from 8 photos and applied to two new photos, 11 kinds: 19 of 22 results ended closer to the true picture, 1 further, 2 declined (photo left unchanged); mean change +6.95 dB.

Heat map: brighter = further from the true image; same scale on every example. Dark means the pixel matches the true picture; full amber means it is off by 32 or more of 255 levels in some colour channel.

Every example is synthetic. Scores are whole-picture PSNR in dB against the true clean image; higher is closer. Engine: Watermark Remover v0.2.8, commit 75a4906.
