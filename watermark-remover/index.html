Honest about what won’t work

What it handles, and what it does not.

Multi-photo mathematical un-blending is the most reliable route when you have several photos carrying the same mark (or a watermark file). For a single photo, an on-device neural model estimates and lifts the mark — convenient, though it may leave faint outlines or occasional damaged patches.

Handles

  • Repeating or tiled semi-transparent watermarks spread across the photo, including batches of mixed-size photos sharing a repeating or corner mark.
  • A mark learned once from several of your photos that carry it (5–8 work best), saved as a watermark file, and removed from new photos — one at a time or many.
  • A single photo (or up to 6 photos when no shared mark is found): the on-device neural model estimates the mark and removes it (median +6.5 dB on our 300-image test set; examples). In Easy mode, a single photo goes straight to this model; tap Not perfect to touch it up by hand.
  • Results that are not right yet: tap Not perfect to refine the learned mark with paint and erase tools, or compare multi-photo and one-photo AI results side by side in Advanced mode.

Does not

  • Photos only — no video. Pick photos rather than video clips.
  • Guarantee a clean result from one photo: the one-photo model improved the picture in most of our tests, but can leave a faint outline and about 1 in 150 test pictures ended with a damaged patch (examples). Learned from 8 photos and saved, single marks did better.
  • Recover what a fully opaque mark hid: that area is filled in by on-device AI (MI-GAN), and the fill is a reconstruction, not the original.
  • Promise results on every kind of mark. Check each result with the wipe before you save it.
Privacy

The photo stays on the phone.

  • Photos and results are processed on the device and never uploaded.
  • Learning and removal need no connection, and the app contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-analytics SDK.
  • Diagnostic and technical reports — crash/out-of-memory/ANR, removal-failure, Hardware Test, self-test, result ratings, comparison choices, and save/share actions — carry no photos and are gated under broad category switches in Settings. Opening the app makes no network connection of its own.
  • Feedback is sent only when you press Send; its attachment checkboxes (including Screenshot) are ticked by default and can show your picture/video if on screen — untick before sending to leave them out.
