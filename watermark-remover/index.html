Photo restoration, on your phone

See the photo, not the stamp.

A watermark tiled across a picture is damage laid over something you already have the right to see. Watermark Remover learns that pattern from your photos and lifts it off, on the phone, without sending them anywhere.

  • On-device
  • Works offline

Honest about what won’t work

What it handles, and what it does not.

The app needs to see the mark more than once: repeating across your photos, on two or more of them. With one copy there is nothing to compare.

Handles

  • Repeating or tiled semi-transparent watermarks spread across the photo.
  • A mark learned from two or more of your photos that carry it. For a single photo, brush the mark out in Quick Remove.
  • Spots the app missed: you can paint over them before it removes anything.

Does not

  • Reliably remove a single standalone logo, such as one mark in a corner. From two or more photos it can try; in our synthetic tests the results ranged from clearly better to badly damaged (see them).
  • Recover what a fully opaque mark hid: that area is filled in, and the fill is a reconstruction, not the original.
  • Promise results on other kinds of marks. This is a beta.
Privacy

The photo stays on the phone.

  • Photos and results are processed on the device and never uploaded.
  • Learning and removal need no connection, and the app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-analytics SDK.
  • Reports the app can send — feedback you write, crash and memory reports, hardware check results — carry no photos, and each has a switch in Settings.
